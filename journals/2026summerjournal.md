## Day 2 - bio ch 20
Reading Bruce Alberts ch 20 (cancer) and brushing up on organic chemistry.
Some very shallow notes/summary: 
- Cancer defined by high reproduction and invasion of resources of healthy cells.
- Carcinoma -> from epithelial cells
- Sarcoma -> from connective tissue / muscle (rarer)
- Myelomas, Leukemias, Lymphomas -> from white blood cells.
- Difference between somatic / germline: somatic is of the body, germline is of sperm and eggs, Cancer usually doesn't get inherited by your parents (although predispositions may). Rather, they occur due to mutations accumulated throughout someone's life. 
- Cancer generally develops gradually through many mutations over time.
